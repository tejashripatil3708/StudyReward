## ✨ Highlights & Features

### 🧸 Mascot Study Companion
* **Cute Mascots with Moods:** Choose from multiple companion buddies (Mochi, Boba, Mimi, Kuma, Pudding) that react to your study habits (Sleeping, Focused, Cheering, Tired).
* **Movable & Draggable Companion:** A floating buddy that hangs out on your screen, fully draggable anywhere so it never blocks your view, and automatically hides when the large home card is visible.
* **Smart Conversational & Math AI:** Built-in intelligence that solves math problems instantly (`2+2`, percentages, algebra, calculus) and answers academic concepts across Physics, Chemistry, Biology, and Coding.

### ⏱️ Focus Timer & Screen Time Economy
* **Earn Screen Time:** Every minute of focus earns you screen time balance to use on your favorite apps.
* **Transparent Reward Ledger:** Comprehensive transaction history showing exactly when minutes were earned, spent, or shared.
* **Custom Balance Tuning:** Adjust earn ratios (1:1 or custom multipliers) and daily limits to match your study goals.

### 🛡️ Smart App & Distraction Blocking
* **Lock Screen Protection:** Automatically locks distracting social media and games when screen time balance expires or during active study sessions.
* **YouTube Shorts Specific Blocker:** Blocks addicting short-form content while keeping educational long-form study videos accessible.
* **Emergency Unlocks & Grace Periods:** Flexible override controls so you're never stranded in an emergency.

### 👥 Study Squads & Community (YPT Style)
* **Discoverable & Private Squads:** Join open public squads or create password-protected private study groups with custom squad codes.
* **Clickable Join Links:** One-tap join links (`https://tinyurl.com/studyreward-invite?code=CODE`) and automatic clipboard detection that prompts you to join the moment you copy a code.
* **Study-With-Me Live Rooms:** Real-time active study indicator showing who is studying right now.
* **Squad Screen Time Pool:** Send and gift earned screen time to motivate friends!

### 📝 Planner, Timetable & To-Do
* **General & Subject Tasks:** Create general daily tasks or categorize them under specific subjects.
* **Timetable Reminders:** Auto-prompts and scheduled focus blocks for classes and study sessions.
* **Calendar Sync:** Seamless event tracking integrated directly into the Plan tab.

## 🚀 Building & Running

### Prerequisites
* Android Studio Ladybug or newer
* Android SDK 35 / 37
* JDK 17 / 21
* Jetpack Compose & Material 3

### Clone & Build
```bash
git clone https://github.com/tejashripatil3708/StudyReward.git
cd StudyReward

# Build Special Edition APK
./gradlew assembleDebug
```
