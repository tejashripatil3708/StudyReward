# Privacy Policy for StudyReward

**Last Updated:** November 5, 2026

StudyReward ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application handles your information.

---

### 1. Information We Collect and Process

#### A. On-Device Usage Data (Screen Time & App Blocking)
* **Usage Stats (`PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS`):** StudyReward reads foreground app usage solely to calculate your earned screen time, determine when time limits have expired, and enforce focus locks. 
* **Local Processing:** **All app usage tracking stays strictly on your local device.** We never upload, transmit, or sell your private app usage history or browsing activity to any external server.

#### B. Accessibility Service (Special Edition Only)
* **Purpose:** In the Special Edition, the optional Accessibility Service is used exclusively to detect when YouTube Shorts are opened (allowing you to block addicting short-form content while keeping educational long-form study videos accessible) and to detect educational channels.
* **No Keylogging or Personal Data Capture:** We do not read typed text, passwords, messages, credit card numbers, or personal sensitive information.

#### C. Account & Social Features (Firebase)
* When you sign in (e.g., via Google Sign-In or guest profile), your user ID, display name, and avatar are stored securely in Google Firebase Firestore.
* If you join or create a **Study Squad**, your focus time totals, active study status, and squad chat messages are synchronized with other members of that squad.

---

### 2. How We Use Your Information
* To track your study sessions and calculate earned screen time rewards.
* To lock distracting apps when your balance expires or during active focus sessions.
* To power Study Squad leaderboards, group study rooms, and squad challenges.
* To provide mascot study companion reactions, offline AI academic problem solving, and timetable reminders.

---

### 3. Data Storage & Security
* **Local Data:** Your study timetable, local task lists, and custom timer preferences are stored securely on your device using encrypted Android shared preferences and local databases.
* **Cloud Security:** Group features and cloud data are secured through Google Firebase enterprise-grade encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest.

---

### 4. Third-Party Services
StudyReward integrates with standard industry services:
* **Google Firebase (Authentication & Firestore Database):** [Firebase Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
* **Google Play Services:** [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal information to third-party data brokers or advertisers.

---

### 5. Children's Privacy
StudyReward is designed for students of all ages, including minors preparing for school and university exams. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13 without parental consent.

---

### 6. Your Rights & Data Deletion
* You can delete your study data or account anytime directly from within the app settings under **Profile > Delete Account & Data**.
* You can revoke app usage or accessibility permissions at any time through your Android System Settings.
